I study how AI systems can make reliable decisions with limited compute and measurements. My work spans physics-informed learning and optimization, active sensing, and efficient inference for large language models.

I develop methods for deciding what to measure, what information to keep, and how to use it in applications such as medical imaging and other inverse problems. I am also interested in bringing these ideas to multimodal and generative AI.
