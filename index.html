Beomgu Kang

I’m a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Korea University, advised by Hyunseok Seo. I develop reliable and resource-efficient AI methods that decide what information to use and how much computation to spend under uncertainty.

During my Ph.D. at KAIST, I developed physics-informed learning methods for quantitative MRI. I used the Bloch equations to optimize acquisition protocols and implemented those protocols on MRI scanners, reducing scan time while preserving quantitative accuracy.

As a postdoc, I extended this work to active sensing, where the model chooses which measurements to acquire based on the input and the downstream task. I now apply the same principle to large language models, studying which KV cache entries to retain to reduce memory use.

In both settings, I study how to set resource budgets based on an acceptable level of risk. How many measurements or how much memory does a model need to perform reliably?
